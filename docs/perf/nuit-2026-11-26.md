# Night of 2026-11-26: toward 30 fps with a right picture (RTX 5080)

Each run: opening cutscene into the cell, `global.sh` harness, fps is the median of the last
20-25 frame lines once the scene is whole. Run-to-run spread is about +-0.6 fps.

## What it says

- **`VICTORY_BVH_NO_HIT=1` (fxceless 96ff135):** +1.2 fps with the picture unchanged. The ray-traced passes no longer walk whatever bytes lie where a structure would be: the structure builder is off.
- **The amplification stage stays on.** With it off (indirect mesh draws clamped by the layer), the fps drops by 2.8.
- **The translator switches `VICTORY_SHADER_NO_ARRAYS`, `FAST_MEM` and `FAST_FLOW` gave no fps.** With them on, heads and the frosted glass went missing. They stay off.
- **Thibault's 4.5 fps regression between 240d464 and 53a0b1a on his RTX 4090 does not show on the 5080**, which is card-bound. It is CPU-side.
- **The blocks along the bottom edge are in every build, the clean base included.** They are older than this work.
- **The light lost in the f15 runs comes from their settings.** With the clean set, the early start of pipelines leaves the light right. The f15 set added `ASYNC_PRESENT`, `QUEUE2`, `HEAP_WORKER`, `ALIAS_ONCE` and the eight branches' switches.

**Loops running to their limit are not what holds the cell scene back.** Cutting every translated loop at 64 makes it no faster. avatarmanz PR #108 found a street shader (39f0e9, grid march) at 12.3 ms for that reason, which may matter outdoors.

**Where the frame goes**, from n1's lines:
- a frame is 91.7 ms;
- the card has nothing of ours queued for 13.5 ms of it, all while the submitting thread is preparing (47.8 ms a frame: copies 23.7, textures 11.2);
- feeding the card without gaps would give about 12.8 fps.
